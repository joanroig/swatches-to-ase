Data controller

Palette Studio is operated by Joan Roig. You can contact us at https://github.com/joanroig/palette-studio/issues.

Local data

Preferences and palettes are stored locally in your browser or device so the app works offline.

Cloud sync data

If you enable cloud sync, we store your account email, display name, avatar colors, palette data, and likes/saves in Firebase Authentication and Firestore. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your profile information with us.

Public palettes

Palettes you publish, along with your display name, are visible to others in Discover.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which features are used, in aggregate. It records events such as creating, publishing or exporting a palette, together with a device identifier and coarse technical data. It never records the contents of your palettes. Analytics is disabled if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

Security

We use Firebase App Check and Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise to prevent abuse. These services may process device and network information (such as IP address) and set security cookies.
